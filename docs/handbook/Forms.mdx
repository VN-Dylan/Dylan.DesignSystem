# Forms

`Form` sets layout and size; `Form.Group` clusters related fields; `Form.Item`
renders the label, required marker, hint and error, and wires them to the
control. Values stay in your form library (react-hook-form, plain state…).

---

## Anatomy

```tsx
<Form id="customer-form" onSubmit={save}>
  <Form.Group title="Contact" description="Who we talk to." columns={2}>
    <Form.Item label="Full name" asterisk errorMessage={errors.name}>
      <Input invalid={!!errors.name} />
    </Form.Item>
    <Form.Item label="Phone" hint="Include the country code.">
      <Input type="tel" />
    </Form.Item>
    <Form.Item label="Notes" span="full" extra="Optional">
      <Input textArea />
    </Form.Item>
  </Form.Group>

  <Form.Group title="Account" columns={2} divided>
    …
  </Form.Group>

  <ActionGroup sticky>
    <Button>Cancel</Button>
    <Button type="submit" variant="solid">
      Save
    </Button>
  </ActionGroup>
</Form>
```

<Markdown>{`
| Part | Does |
| --- | --- |
| \`Form layout\` | \`vertical\` (default), \`horizontal\` (label column — stacks on mobile), \`inline\`. |
| \`Form.Group\` | A \`fieldset\` with a \`legend\` title and optional \`description\` / \`extra\`. \`columns\` 1–4: one column on mobile, 2 from \`sm\`, 3–4 from \`lg\`. \`divided\` draws a hairline above. \`disabled\` locks every field inside (read-only views). |
| \`Form.Item\` | Label, \`asterisk\`, \`hint\` (replaced by \`errorMessage\` while invalid), \`span\` (2/3/4/\`full\`) inside a group. A single control child receives \`id\` and \`aria-describedby\` automatically. |
`}</Markdown>

## Rules

- **Group by meaning, not by size.** Two to six fields per group; title it
  with a noun ("Contact", "Billing").
- **Columns only for short, related fields** (first / last name, city / zip).
  Long text, addresses and notes take `span="full"`.
- **Required fields get `asterisk`;** don't mark optional ones except with
  `extra="Optional"` when most fields are required.
- **Validate on submit, clear on edit.** Show the error under the field and
  move focus to the first invalid one.
- **Actions** follow **Handbook/Actions & Buttons**: `ActionGroup sticky` on a
  page; the `footer` prop of `Dialog` / `Drawer` (with `form="<id>"` on the
  submit button) in an overlay.
- **Read-only access:** render the same form with `Form.Group disabled` and
  replace Save with a "Read-only access" note — don't build a second view.
